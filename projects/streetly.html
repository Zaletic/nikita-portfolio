Streetly

Helps you compare. choose. ride.

A mobile app that brings every e-bike rental provider into one map, so commuters can compare bikes, pricing and battery levels and book the right ride without switching apps.

01 · Overview

One platform for every e-bike nearby

Streetly is a mobile application that simplifies the process of renting e-bikes by bringing multiple rental providers onto a single platform. Instead of downloading and comparing several provider-specific apps or websites, users can discover nearby bikes, compare availability, pricing, battery levels and features, and book the option that suits them best.

02 · Problem

Rentals are fragmented, and so is the search

Urban commuters increasingly rely on e-bike rentals for short-distance travel, but existing services are fragmented and provider-specific. Users struggle to find available bikes, compare options and understand pricing across platforms. This creates unnecessary friction, making rentals less efficient and more time-consuming.

  • 01Existing platforms are fragmented
  • 02Users face challenges locating and comparing rental services around them
  • 03Limited flexibility in booking
03 · Research

Understanding the commuter

Before designing Streetly, I explored how people navigate short-distance travel, what influences their choice of transport and what they need to know before renting an e-bike. Interviews, surveys and secondary research framed the opportunity: making e-bike rental options easier to discover, compare and book.

60survey respondents
8–10interview participants

The research covered travel frequency, rental discovery, pricing preferences and the information users need before booking a ride.

01

Know before you ride

Users need to assess whether a bike has sufficient battery for their intended journey before booking.

Design implication

Display battery status and estimated riding range on the bike details screen, before users commit to a booking.

02

Compare without switching

Finding and comparing rental options across separate provider platforms adds friction to the discovery process.

Design implication

Bring multiple providers into one discovery experience, making availability, pricing, and range easier to compare.

03

Know what you'll pay

Users need clarity on ride costs and available payment options to make informed booking decisions.

Design implication

Show a transparent fare breakdown, including the selected ride duration and applicable charges, before payment.

Mode of transport

Thinks

Independence

Desires freedom from relying on Rapido / Ola / Uber

Cost effective

Efficiency

Transport must save time and avoid traffic or petrol pump queues

Trial period

Believes they should get a “free trial” or “one kilometer free” before committing to a subscription

Feels

Unaware

Feels a “lack of awareness” about available rental options in the area

Anxious

Pressured

Hesitant

Insight

Insight
