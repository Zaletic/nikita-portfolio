Overview

Streetly is a mobile application that simplifies the process of renting e-bikes by bringing multiple rental providers onto a single platform.

Instead of downloading and comparing several provider-specific apps and websites, users can discover nearby bikes, compare availability, pricing, battery levels and features, and book the option that best suits their needs.

Problem Statement

Urban commuters increasingly rely on e-bike rentals for short-distance travel, but existing services are fragmented and provider-specific.

Users struggle to find available bikes, compare options, and understand pricing across different platforms. This fragmented experience creates unnecessary friction, making rentals less efficient and more time-consuming.

04

Understanding the Commuter

Before designing Streetly, I explored how people navigate short-distance travel, what influences their choice of transport, and what they need to know before renting an e-bike.

Through interviews, surveys, and secondary research, I explored the challenges around discovering rental options, evaluating costs, and making quick, informed travel decisions.

The research helped frame the opportunity for Streetly: making e-bike rental options easier to discover, compare, and book.

05

Insights

To understand commuting habits, transport preferences, and expectations around e-bike rentals. The research explored travel frequency, rental discovery, pricing preferences, and the information users need before booking a ride.

60 Survey respondents

8-10 Interview participants

Know before you ride

Users need to assess whether a bike has sufficient battery for their intended journey before booking.

Design implication

Display battery status and estimated riding range on the bike details screen, before users commit to a booking.

Compare without switching

Finding and comparing rental options across separate provider platforms adds friction to the discovery process.

Design implication

Bring multiple providers into one discovery experience, making availability, pricing, and range easier to compare.

Know what you’ll pay

Users need clarity on ride costs and available payment options to make informed booking decisions.

Design implication

Showing a transparent fare breakdown, including the selected ride duration and applicable charges, before payment.

Insights

Users need transparent information (battery, pricing, availability) before booking.

Switching between multiple rental apps creates unnecessary friction.

Speed and flexibility matter more than brand loyalty.

The Solution

A unified e-bike rental platform that brings multiple service providers into one seamless experience.

Users can easily discover nearby bikes, compare pricing, availability, and battery levels, and book the most suitable ride without switching between multiple apps.

  • 01

    Compare multiple rental providers in one app.

  • 02

    View live bike availability, battery levels, and pricing before booking.

  • 03

    Book the best ride in just a few taps with a seamless rental experience.

13

Explore & Compare

The Explore screen provides a live map of nearby e-bike providers, enabling users to quickly discover and compare available options.

Provider Details screen presents key information such as availability, amenities, battery status, and bike models to support informed booking decisions.

14

Choose & Schedule

The booking screen allows users to select their preferred bike, ride duration, and reservation time with a simple and flexible interface.

The payment screen provides a transparent fare breakdown and multiple payment options, ensuring a smooth and confident booking experience.

15

After the Booking

Once the booking is confirmed, users receive a clear reservation summary with pickup details, countdown timer, and quick actions

The Bookings screen provides easy access to active and past reservations, allowing users to track and manage their rides effortlessly.

The Past Bookings screen allows users to revisit previous rides, view booking history, and quickly rebook frequent trips

The Profile screen centralizes personal information, verified documents, emergency contacts, and support services for a secure and personalized experience.
