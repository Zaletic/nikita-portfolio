The existing ecosystem

Why does grooming become stressful?

Grooming is a loop, not a line: prepare, position, groom, manage, reward, continue. Each time the dog gets restless the owner has to stop, reward and refocus, and the cycle repeats many times in one session.

The reality

Grooming and engagement compete for the owner's attention. Treats and sounds help, but they are manual, inconsistent and interrupt the flow.

What both need

The dog: calm, comfort and something to focus on.
The owner: to groom effectively without constant interruption.

What the research says

Sound and colour can hold a dog's attention

Music during grooming

Calmer behaviour

Krupa et al., 2025 observed 15 companion dogs during bathing, drying, clipping and nail trimming. Dogs were calmer when classical music was played than with no music.

Different music genres

Less stress

Bowman et al., 2017 exposed 38 kennelled dogs to classical, soft rock, reggae, pop and Motown. Music led to more resting and less standing, with some genres stronger. Read the paper

How dogs see colour

Yellow gets noticed

Roy et al., 2025 found dogs approached a yellow object first, even when a grey one held food. Blue and yellow sit in their sensitive range. Read the paper

Yellow can attract the attention of Indian free-ranging dogs.

05 · Prototype

The electronics behind it

An Arduino controls three outputs: an LED that glows yellow and blue, a speaker that plays calming audio, and a solenoid that releases a treat when the groomer presses the button.

06 · What's next

Future versions and roadmap

Today · GroomCue

Assisted engagement

Light, sound and a treat: one-click treat dispensing, yellow and blue LED, integrated audio and adjustable height, all controlled by the groomer.

Next · Smart GroomCue

Adaptive engagement

Sensing movement or loss of attention, adjusting reward timing and audio, and offering calm, reward and grooming modes.

Later · Connected GroomCue

Personalised companion

A mobile app with dog profiles for multiple dogs, treat tracking, saved preferences, favourite audio and session history.

Because of prototype constraints, this version focuses on the core interaction: continuous light and sound, with manually triggered treats.
