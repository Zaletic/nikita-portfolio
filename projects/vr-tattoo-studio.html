VR Tattoo Studio

VR Tattoo Studio reimagines the tattoo parlour as an immersive space for cultural learning. Users step from a virtual studio through a portal into the world of a Māori tribe of New Zealand, meeting its culture first-hand, then return carrying a tattoo on their hand as a memory of the journey.

The idea

From studio visit to cultural journey.

A tattoo studio is usually a transactional space: a person arrives, chooses a design and leaves. This project asks whether the visit can become something richer. Set in virtual reality, the studio turns interactive and doubles as a doorway, inviting users to learn about the cultural roots of tattooing by stepping into a world where that culture lives.

The experience
01

Arrival

Users begin inside a virtual tattoo studio, a familiar setting they can look around and interact with. From here they are invited to choose a world to enter.

02

Immersion

Choosing the Māori world transports users to the setting of a Māori tribe of New Zealand. Objects and surroundings drawn from the culture appear in context, so understanding builds through exploration rather than explanation.

03

Return

A portal leads back to the studio. The journey leaves a trace: a tattoo on the user's hand, a lasting memory of the place they visited.

Decisions

Interaction over passive viewing

To deepen engagement, the studio became a space to explore and make choices in, giving users agency from the very first moment.

Learning through presence

Tattoos carry cultural meaning. Rather than explaining it in text, the experience places that context in an environment users inhabit, so knowledge is encountered, not lectured.

The tattoo as memory

Ending with a tattoo on the user's hand closes the loop between story and studio. The mark becomes a personal souvenir that links the virtual journey to the act of being tattooed.

How it was made

Built with Blender and Unity.

The 3D spaces were modelled in Blender and assembled in Unity, where the interactions, the portal transition and the VR experience were developed.
