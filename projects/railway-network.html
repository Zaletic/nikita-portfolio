01 · Brief

Mapping the Central Indian Railway Network

Project objectiveTo explore how railway network information can be transformed into a clear, structured, and visually engaging digital experience using information design.

The project explores how railway network information can be organised and communicated through an interactive digital experience. By combining geographical mapping, structured data, and visual representation, I explored ways to make the network easier to navigate and understand.

The outcome is a website concept that brings railway information into a unified visual experience.

02 · Design challenge

Making complex data easier to understand

Railway information exists across different geographical and administrative layers. Presenting these relationships in a single view can become visually overwhelming when every piece of information competes for attention.

  1. 01Routes connect stations
  2. 02Divisions organise railway operations
  3. 03Larger networks link different regions

The challenge: translate this complexity into a coherent visual language that communicates the structure of the network while keeping the information organised and approachable.
