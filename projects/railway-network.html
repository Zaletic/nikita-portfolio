A railway map you can read in one scroll.

Routes, divisions and zones all compete for attention. This project turns them into a calm, layered website for the Central Indian railway network.

Brief

Mapping the Central Indian Railway Network

Project objective

To explore how railway network information can be transformed into a clear, structured and visually engaging digital experience using information design.

By combining geographical mapping, structured data and visual representation, I explored ways to make the network easier to navigate and understand. The outcome is a website concept that brings railway information into one unified visual experience.

Design challenge

Making complex data easier to understand.

Railway information exists across geographical and administrative layers. Routes connect stations, divisions organise operations, and larger networks link regions. Showing all of it in one view becomes overwhelming when every piece competes for attention.

The challenge was to translate this complexity into a coherent visual language that communicates the structure of the network while keeping the information organised and approachable.

Research and understanding

Understanding the network.

Before the visual direction, I looked into the structure of the network: its geographical spread, divisions, routes and history.

Information architecture

Organizing the information.

One scroll, six sections. Pick a stop and the train takes you there.

Zones

The network is split into 17 railway zones, each with its own headquarters and a set of divisions.

Visual exploration

Initial exploration.

The exploration focused on how these elements could be represented consistently, while keeping a clear distinction between the layers of information.

This stage established a visual direction for the website, considering how layout, hierarchy, mapping and graphical elements could work together.

The website

The final website.

The website is the primary outcome of the project: a digital space for presenting the railway network through maps, structured information and visual exploration.
