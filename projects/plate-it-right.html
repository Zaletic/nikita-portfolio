An exploration

A first try at augmented reality.

This was an exploration, not a finished product: a short exercise to learn how AR works by building one with Kivicube, a browser-based WebAR tool. The result runs straight from a link on a phone, with no app to install.

Pointing the camera at a printed plate image brings up “Plate It Right”: a 3D plate that splits into food groups, showing what a balanced meal looks like. Half the plate is vegetables and fruits, a quarter is carbs and a quarter is protein.

Screen recording on a phone (about 25 seconds).

What I explored

Image tracking and placing content in space.

The exercise was about the basics of AR: setting up an image marker for the camera to recognise, anchoring 3D content to it, and labelling it so it can be read at a glance while the phone moves.
